    How it works

    From schedule to results, step by step

    Follow one race through AvilonX.

    1. 1

      Schedule the race

      Pick the release point, release time, open and end dates. Distances to every loft are worked out from GPS coordinates.

    2. 2

      Register the birds

      Enter each pigeon with its RFID tag or race sticker. Only bonded birds can be entered, so every entry belongs to the right member.

    3. 3

      Release and forecast

      Once the birds are released, members see a speed forecast: when a bird flying 1,200 m/min should be home.

    4. 4

      Clock the arrival

      The loft RFID reader, an Android phone with NFC, or the sticker scratch code. Each clocking gets the server's time, not the phone's.

    5. 5

      Follow race day live

      Arrivals, speeds and loft reader status update as they happen. Put it on the big screen at the club.

    6. 6

      Verify show up

      Members bring clocked birds to the club. Tap the tag to verify; birds not shown before the race closes are disqualified.

    7. 7

      Publish the results

      Ranked by speed, exported to Excel or PDF, and open to the public by club and race date.

    Features

    Everything a racing club needs

    • Server-timed clocking

      Every clocking is stamped by the server, so results are fair.

    • RFID and NFC

      Wi-Fi and USB loft readers, or an Android phone's NFC.

    • Stickers and Eclock

      Scratch codes for clubs without readers.

    • Live race day

      Arrivals and speeds the moment birds are clocked.

    • Show up checks

      Verify clocked birds at the club before the race closes.

    • Excel and PDF

      Export entries, race day and results in one click.

    • Members and team

      Accounts for admins, staff and members, with change history.

    • Many clubs

      Each club has its own data, members and logo.

AvilonX - ACR122U reader helper (Windows)

Nothing to register, no key.

1. Unzip this folder (right-click > Extract All). Do not run it from inside the zip.
2. Plug in the ACR122U and double-click "Start ACR122U for AvilonX.cmd".
   Keep its window open.
3. In AvilonX (Race entries, Bird bonding, Show up) choose "Wired" as the reader.
   The page shows "ACR122U helper connected".
   If the browser asks to allow access to devices on your network, choose Allow.
4. Tap tags on the ACR122U.

Other option: "Start ACR122U keyboard helper.cmd" types each tag into the window
in front (click the browser page after starting it).

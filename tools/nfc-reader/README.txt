AvilonX - ACR122U reader helper (Windows)

Nothing to register, no key.

EASIEST: install it once
1. Unzip this folder (right-click > Extract All). Do not run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click "Install AvilonX ACR122U helper.cmd".
   The helper starts now (minimized on the taskbar) and every time you sign in
   to Windows. There is also an "AvilonX ACR122U" icon on the Desktop.
3. In AvilonX (Race entries, Bird bonding, Show up, Clocking) choose "Wired".
   The page shows "ACR122U helper connected".
   If the browser asks to allow access to devices on your network, choose Allow.
4. Tap tags on the ACR122U.
   Two tags on the reader at the same time are both read. More than two: lift
   them off and tap again. Each tag counts once while it stays on the reader.
To remove it: "Uninstall AvilonX ACR122U helper.cmd".

Without installing: double-click "Start ACR122U for AvilonX.cmd" and keep its
window open while you use the reader.

Old option: "Start ACR122U keyboard helper.cmd" types each tag into the window
in front. Do not run it together with the AvilonX helper.
